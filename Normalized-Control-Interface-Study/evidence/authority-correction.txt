Consequences:
  - nu_yaw = +1 is not reachable within the positive relative yaw authority;
    nu_yaw = -1 lies inside the negative relative authority.
  - nu in [-1,1] is neither absolute full deflection nor full relative authority;
    it is the PX4 pseudo-inverse column-conditioning interval.
  - PX4 control_trim = B * linearization_point (normally zero); actuator_trim =
    trim + linearization_point. The allocated control is relative to actuator_trim,
    so the absolute trim torque is a baseline, not part of the relative setpoint.
