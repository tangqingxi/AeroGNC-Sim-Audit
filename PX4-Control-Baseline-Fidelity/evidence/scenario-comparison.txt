Final remediation changes:
  - default decoupled body-z: z_b = [-ax,-ay,g]/norm (vertical acceleration
    does not change tilt);
  - PX4 hover-thrust SI mapping T = T_hover*(1-az/g)/cos with
    T_hover=trim.thrust_n;
  - horizontal reserve min(actual demand, margin) instead of unconditional
    full margin;
  - final limited force vector determines both q and thrust_n; Tracking AW
    uses the final force.

The hover final error improves slightly; max error is unchanged because it is
dominated by the initial transient. Rate Step bypasses Position/Velocity and
therefore is unchanged (as expected), confirming the Velocity change does not
silently retune the rate loop.
